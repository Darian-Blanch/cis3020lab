My Top 5

  • Characters
    •
    1. Gojo
      • One of the few sorcers ranked as a Special Grade. Considered the strongest sorcerer of the modern era.
    2. Yuta
      • One of the few sorcerers ranked as a Special Grade. Main protagonist of the prequel movie.
    3. Sukuna
      • Sorcerer from an ancient time known as the Heian Era. Also known as the King of Curses.
    4. Yuji
      • Main protagonist of the series. Vessel of Sukuna. Heavily inspired by Buddhism references.
    5. Todo
      • Also known as Yuji's brother. Most relevant character from the Kyto branch of Jujutsu High.
