How did you become a fan?

I began watching the anime shortly after its release.

What do you love most about it?

I love the power system and worldbuilding most because of how much there is to the story.

What would you recommend to someone just getting started?

I would recommend watching the movie "Jujutsu Kaisen 0" anytime before season 2, but sooner is better.

Do you have any favorite fan communities or creators?

I enjoy Adam McArthur, the dub voice actor for Yuji Itadori, as a creator because of how involved he is with the community on social media and conventions.
